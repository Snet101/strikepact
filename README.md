# StrikePact

StrikePact is a planned wearable and companion app to help students follow through on commitments and manage procrastination.

## Project Status

**Planning and research only. Development has not started, and there is no working app or hardware prototype yet.**

I proposed StrikePact for a business-class assignment and began researching wristband designs, potential features, and student interest.

## Features Under Consideration

- **Task commitments:** Let students define what they want to complete.
- **Task verification:** Explore how students could confirm completion. The verification method has not been decided.
- **Haptic reminders:** Use wristband vibrations as physical reminders connected to commitments in the app.
- **Optional app blocking:** Explore whether users could limit distracting apps during a task, depending on device permissions and technical feasibility.

These are proposed features, not implemented functionality or a finalized product specification.

## Current Design Questions

- Which features belong in the first version?
- What should the app’s interface and user flow look like?
- Which frontend and backend technologies fit those requirements?
- How should the app communicate with the wristband?
- What band design could accommodate the necessary electronics while remaining comfortable and affordable?

No technology stack or hardware design has been finalized.

## Initial Student Research

I surveyed 82 students about the concept:
- 35 said they would definitely buy it.
- 37 said maybe.

These responses helped me explore interest, but do not establish willingness to pay or long-term use.

## Next Steps

Narrow the initial feature set, sketch the app’s user flow, investigate app-to-band communication, and assess hardware costs before choosing an approach for a prototype.
